1 Introduction

[hide]

AstroClean removes residual dust shadows, diffuse reflections and other background defects from linear astronomical images using a synthetic flat. The object mask protects genuine astronomical structures from being included in the flat. AstroClean complements, rather than replaces, normal calibration and gradient correction.

2 Requirements

[hide]

Use PixInsight 1.9.6 with V8 and native MultiscaleMedianTransform (MMT). Open a linear Original and a matching linear Starless image with identical geometry and channels. Do not permanently stretch either image. For an existing flat, Starless is optional.

3 Quick start

[hide]

  1. Select Original and Starless and click Run AstroClean.
  2. Inspect the automatic green object mask. Add missing galaxies or nebulosity; remove any protected dust shadows or reflections.
  3. Click Calculate and inspect Corrected and Synthetic flat.
  4. Adjust the mask or parameters and calculate again if necessary.
  5. Click Finish to open the output images. Save them in PixInsight if required.

4 Mask editor

[hide]

Green areas protect real objects. Use Add to mask or Remove from mask and draw a lasso. Undo mask edit reverses the last edit. Mask On/Off changes overlay visibility only; Mask only displays the protection mask. Mouse wheel zooms; right or middle drag pans; Fit resets the view. Original and Corrected select the before/after image; Stars On/Off switches the corresponding preview. Background and Highlights adjust display stretch only, not the linear data.

5 Parameters

[hide]

ParameterPurpose
Mask sensitivity (0–100, default 50)Higher values generally protect more faint structure. Click Update mask to apply and replay manual lasso edits.
MMT layers + R (1–6, default 2)Number of suppressed detail layers in the flat model. Higher values produce a smoother model but can lose small dust shadows.
Edge soften (0–128 px, default 0)Smooths the filled boundary without changing the binary mask. Try 30–50 pixels if a boundary is visible.
Flat resolution (25%, 50%, 100%; default 25%)Higher resolution can retain smaller defects at greater processing cost.

Click Calculate after changing flat parameters.

6 Synthetic flat calculation

[hide]

Protected areas are reconstructed from surrounding background using regularized thin-plate spline (TPS) interpolation. Boundary softening and MMT filtering form the flat model. The corrected image is computed per channel as Original × median(flat) / flat. Dust shadows and reflections should remain visible in the flat, while real astronomical objects should not. Inspect both outputs; a synthetic flat is an estimate, not a measured calibration flat.

7 Local Remove

[hide]

Select Local Remove, draw a lasso around an unwanted feature, and click Remove. TPS reconstructs the local background on the starless working image, excluding protected areas from background sampling. The additive difference is transferred to the image with stars, with an approximately 16-pixel internal feather. No synthetic flat is generated or changed by this operation. Undo Remove restores the preceding result (one step). Further removals can be applied successively. Calculate is disabled after a removal; restart with the original inputs for a fresh flat if needed. Real structures within the selection can also be removed; missing stars or hidden detail cannot be reconstructed. Use a small margin around the defect.

8 Resume from mask

[hide]

Open Original, Starless and the saved grayscale mask with matching dimensions. Select Existing mask and click Resume from mask. Edit the protection and calculate a new flat.

9 Apply existing flat

[hide]

Open Original and a matching linear synthetic flat, including one edited externally. Select Existing synthetic flat and click Apply existing flat. The supplied flat is used directly at full resolution, without smoothing or regeneration. Dimensions and channels must match and all flat samples must be finite and positive. Starless is optional. Mask editing, Calculate and Local Remove are disabled. Finish opens only the corrected image.

10 Output images

[hide]

Finish opens a new image named after Original with the suffix _AstroClean. A calculated mask and synthetic flat are also opened with _Mask and _SyntheticFlat suffixes. If Local Remove follows Calculate, the previously calculated mask and flat are retained; they do not represent the additive local repair. Local Remove alone produces only the repaired image. Cancel discards unpublished results. Source images are not overwritten; save outputs explicitly.

11 Tips and limitations

[hide]

If a dust shadow remains, check that it is not protected by the mask and try a higher flat resolution or fewer MMT layers. If galaxy structure appears in the flat, expand the mask to include faint outer regions. For large objects occupying most of the frame, surrounding background may be insufficient for reliable interpolation. Inspect the result at a consistent stretch. Local Remove is intended for localized defects, not recovery of obscured astronomical detail.
